# Outreach templates

Fill every `{...}` from the data. Delete any line you cannot fill truthfully. Keep `[...]` for the
person to complete (offers, dates, prices you do not know). Rewrite freely: these are shapes, not
scripts. Body under 120 words.

## Follow-up

Use after a purchase, a quote or an unanswered question.

```
Subject: {product or topic}: next step?

Hi {first name},

{One sentence on the specific thing: the {product} you picked up on {date}, or the quote for {topic}.}
{One sentence of value: a tip for using it, the answer to their open question, or what happens next.}

{One clear ask: "Would Thursday or Friday work for a quick call?" / "Want me to hold the slot?"}

Thanks,
{sign-off}
```

## Win-back

Use when they bought before and nothing in 90+ days. Warm, no guilt, no "we miss you!!!".

```
Subject: {something new or relevant to what they bought}

Hi {first name},

It's been a while since your {last product} in {month}. {One honest line: what is new that fits
them, or a question about how {last product} worked out.}

{Optional, only if the person supplied one: [offer, e.g. 10% off your next order until {date}].}

{Low-pressure ask: "If it's useful, reply and I'll put some options together."}

Best,
{sign-off}
```

## Thank-you

Use after a purchase, a big order, a referral, a review or a milestone (first year, 10th order).

```
Subject: Thank you, {first name}

Hi {first name},

Thank you for {the specific thing: your order of {product} / sending {referral} our way / the kind
review}. {One personal line: why it matters to a small business, or a detail about their order.}

{Optional helpful line: care tip, what to expect next, who to contact.}

No reply needed - just wanted you to know it's appreciated.

{sign-off}
```

## Reply to inbound

Use when their latest message is `direction = 'in'`. Answer first, then context.

```
Subject: Re: {their subject}

Hi {first name},

{Direct answer to their question in one or two sentences.}
{Supporting detail from the data: order {product} on {date}, status, next step.}
{If you cannot answer: "I'm checking [what] and will get back to you by [day]."}

{Close: "Anything else, just reply here."}

{sign-off}
```

## Examples

Win-back for a bakery owner whose last order was "Wholesale flour 25kg" on 2026-07-01:

```
Subject: New stone-ground flour for your bakery

Hi Sam,

It's been a few months since your wholesale flour order in July. We've just started milling a
stone-ground rye that a few bakeries are using for weekend loaves, and I thought of you.

If it's useful, reply and I'll send a sample bag with your next order.

Best,
Jo
```

Reply to inbound "Do you ship to Canada?" from a customer with a purchase last month:

```
Subject: Re: Shipping to Canada

Hi Priya,

Yes, we ship to Canada. Orders usually arrive in [5-8] business days and duties are [included].

Your last order (the linen apron set) went out from our Portland studio, so the same options apply.
Anything else, just reply here.

Jo
```
