# Reply macros

Start from the macro, then make it personal: their name, their order or product, their exact
problem in one line. Fill `{...}` from the data and leave `[...]` for the person (policies,
dates, amounts you do not know). Under 120 words. One apology at most, and only when we got
something wrong.

## Acknowledge and investigate (urgent or escalated)

```
Hi {first name},

Thanks for flagging this - I can see {one-line restatement of the problem}. I'm looking into it
now and will update you by [time today].

{If money is involved: "If you were charged twice, the extra charge will be refunded in full - you
won't need to do anything else." Only if the person confirmed the refund policy.}

{sign-off}
```

## Double charge / billing error

```
Hi {first name},

You're right - I can see two charges of {amount} on {date} for {order/product}. I'm sorry about
that. [I've asked for the duplicate to be refunded; it usually shows on your statement in 5-10
business days.]

I'll confirm here once it's processed.

{sign-off}
```

## Where is my order

```
Hi {first name},

Your order of {product} from {purchase date} [shipped on {date} with {carrier}, tracking {number}].
{If late: "It's running later than it should - I'm checking with the carrier and will update you
by [day]."}

{If they have a deadline: "I've noted you need it by {date} and I'm treating this as a priority."}

{sign-off}
```

## How-to

```
Hi {first name},

Good question. To {goal}:

1. {step}
2. {step}
3. {step}

If anything looks different on your screen, reply with a screenshot and I'll walk you through it.

{sign-off}
```

## Bug report

```
Hi {first name},

Thanks for the details - that helps. I've reproduced / logged {short description of the bug}.
{Workaround, if one exists: "In the meantime, you can {workaround}."}

I'll let you know when it's fixed.

{sign-off}
```

## Feature request

```
Hi {first name},

Thanks for the idea - {restate it in one line}. I've added it to our list. I can't promise a date,
but requests like this are exactly how we decide what to build next.

{sign-off}
```

## Refund request (needs a person's decision)

```
Hi {first name},

Thanks for getting in touch about {product}. I've passed your refund request to [name], who will
reply by [day]. {Do not promise or refuse the refund.}

{sign-off}
```

## Account or data request

```
Hi {first name},

Got it - you'd like to {change email / export your data / delete your account}. To keep your
account safe, [please reply from the email on the account / confirm the last 4 digits of the card
used]. Once confirmed, we'll complete it within [N] days.

{sign-off}
```
