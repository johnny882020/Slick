Scope note on method: 17 search/fetch calls. Reddit (reddit.com) was **not accessible** to the search tool ("domains are not accessible to our user agent"), so there are no direct Reddit quotes in this file. User voice comes from Trustpilot (fetched directly), plus G2/Capterra snippets surfaced through search. Many numbers come from vendor blogs that cite surveys I could not locate. ## Q1. Which jobs eat unpaid hours or lose revenue, and how much?

### Takeaway
Measured figures put admin at roughly 3 to 6 hours a week, and all non-billable work at roughly 6 to 15 hours a week. The largest *revenue* leak that is quantifiable but poorly tooled is scope creep (unpaid extra work), reported at about 12 unpaid hours a month, but those figures rest on weak, mostly vendor-sourced evidence. For therapists, insurance work (denials, credentialing) is the clearest unpaid-hours sink.

### Cited Findings
**Size of the segment (context)**
- US independents: 72.9M in 2025. Full-time independents were flat at 27.6M. A record 5.6M earned >$100k/yr, about double 2020. Fielded April 2025 (MBO Partners is a vendor to independents, so the survey is vendor-sponsored) — [MBO Partners 2025 State of Independence](https://www.mbopartners.com/state-of-independence/2025-report)
- 74% of independents used generative AI in 2025, versus 69% of traditional workers — [MBO Partners 2025](https://www.mbopartners.com/state-of-independence/2025-report)
- Upwork estimates 1 in 4 US skilled knowledge workers work independently, earning $1.5T (Upwork-sponsored) — [Upwork press release](https://www.upwork.com/press/releases/upwork-study-finds-1-in-4-u-s-skilled-knowledge-workers-now-work-independently-generating-1-5-trillion-in-earnings)
- Upwork Future Workforce Index 2026 (2,400 US skilled workers, Mar to Apr 2026): freelancers using AI earn 34% more per hour. Gen-AI/creative production contract starts rose 90% YoY, but per-contract earnings fell 13%. AI-augmented professional services volume rose 72% and earnings rose 22% (Upwork-sponsored) — [Upwork research](https://www.upwork.com/research/research-future-workforce-index-2026); [Allwork summary, Jul 2026](https://allwork.space/2026/07/skilled-freelancing-jumps-36-as-ai-rewards-expertise-over-routine-work/)

**Admin and non-billable hours**
- About 6.2 hrs/week on scheduling and admin, about 35% of time non-billable. Attributed to a Calendly freelancer survey I could not check **[vendor/unverified]** — [SchedulingKit stats](https://schedulingkit.com/statistics/freelancer-statistics)
- Freelancer Map survey: 47% of freelancers spend 10 to 20% of their time on administration and accounting. Clockify's own estimate is about 6 hrs/week of admin (Clockify is a time-tracking vendor) — [Clockify 2025](https://clockify.me/how-freelancers-spend-time)
- About 5,000 self-reported Jobbers.io users (2024 to 2025): about 15.3 non-billable hrs/week, of which about 3.2 hrs is admin, invoicing and contracts (platform-sourced) — [Jobbers.io](https://www.jobbers.io/how-much-time-do-freelancers-waste-weekly-data-from-5000-jobbers-io-users/)
- Smallpdf 2026 (397 freelancers): an average of 204 hrs/year lost to admin and paperwork "even with AI tools". This is a vendor survey, surfaced via search summary, and I did not fetch the primary — [search result via Clockify/aggregators; primary not located]

**Scope creep (revenue leak)**
- "68% of freelancers experience scope creep on at least half their projects, average 12 unpaid hours/month", attributed to a "2025 Freelance Forward survey". I could not confirm the original, and Upwork's Freelance Forward 2025 report did not surface **[vendor/unverified]** — [Damongo guide](https://damongo.com/how-to-stop-scope-creep-as-a-freelancer-in-2026-complete-guide-protecting-time-rates/)
- "67% do unpaid work because of scope creep (2026 Freelancers Union study)" **[unverified attribution]**. "53% named scope creep the biggest profitability challenge (2025 Payoneer)" **[unverified]** — [eonebill.ai](https://www.eonebill.ai/blog/how-to-prevent-scope-creep-as-a-freelancer); [stopscopecreep.com stats page](https://stopscopecreep.com/blog/scope-creep-statistics) (the fetch failed with HTTP 522, so the content comes from the search snippet only)
- Estimated loss of $2,000 to $5,000/yr per active freelancer, "likely an undercount since most don't track it" **[vendor estimate]** — [stopscopecreep.com](https://stopscopecreep.com/blog/scope-creep-statistics). One guide works out 12 hrs/mo × $75/hr = $6,480/yr — [Damongo](https://damongo.com/how-to-stop-scope-creep-as-a-freelancer-in-2026-complete-guide-protecting-time-rates/)
- IndieHackers post framing a "$10k to $20k profit leak" from unbilled or underpriced hours — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/the-hidden-10k-20k-profit-leak-many-freelancers-never-calculate-4851151213) (title only; not fetched)

**Therapists (insurance admin)**
- In-network work adds non-billable hours for coding, denials and payer audits. Some therapists go self-pay to avoid this (SimplePractice's own blog) — [SimplePractice](https://www.simplepractice.com/blog/should-i-get-paneled-with-insurance-payers/)
- Credentialing takes 90 to 180 days per payer. Denials often trace to enrollment status ("provider not enrolled"), not the claim itself, and staff resubmit without finding the root cause (vendor RCM sources) — [Clarity Health RCM](https://www.clarityhealthrcm.com/insights/insurance-credentialing-for-therapists); [Your Health Magazine](https://yourhealthmagazine.net/article/healthcare-administration/how-credentialing-gaps-turn-into-unpaid-claims-in-mental-health-practices/); [ICANotes, Feb 2026](https://www.icanotes.com/2026/02/10/insurance-credentialing-pitfalls-mental-health/)
- "A single unpaid claim can ripple into hours of phone calls and resubmissions" (Blueprint, a vendor) — [Blueprint](https://www.blueprint.ai/blog/insurance-claim-denials)

**Marketplace lead-gen cost (Upwork)**
- Upwork has used a variable 0 to 15% freelancer fee since May 2025, set by Upwork per contract, with most contracts near 10%. Connects cost $0.15 each, a proposal can cost "a couple to more than a dozen" Connects, and Connects are "the only fee you pay whether or not you win". Freelancer Plus is $19.99/mo — [Monitask](https://www.monitask.com/blog/upwork-fees/); [LevelUpUpwork](https://www.levelupupwork.com/blog/upwork-fees-explained); [getmany](https://getmany.com/blog/upwork-fees)

### Inferences
- Admin hours (3 to 6 hrs/week) are real but diffuse. Every incumbent already claims to "save admin time", so a product pitched on admin time alone will be undifferentiated. The pains that convert to *money* are better wedges: unbilled scope changes, lost or denied insurance claims, and proposals that never close.
- Upwork's own data says low-complexity execution work is commoditising (-13% per-contract earnings). That pushes freelancers toward higher-ticket, scoped professional services, where scoping, proposals and change control matter more.

### Gaps
- No primary-source, independent measurement of scope-creep losses. All 52 to 68% figures trace to blogs citing surveys I could not find. **Validation needed via the founder's own interviews.**
- I could not reach Reddit (r/freelance, r/consulting, r/therapists) for verbatim posts.
- The IPSE (UK) and European Freelancers survey 2025/26 data did not surface. I found no EU-specific figures on hours lost.
- No primary data on time spent on proposals, discovery calls or unpaid pitching.

## Q2. What do they already pay for, and what do reviews say is still broken?

### Takeaway
This segment pays $20 to $180/month per tool and is **highly price-sensitive**. HoneyBook's 89% hike and SimplePractice's 41 to 69% hikes created visible churn intent. Reviews complain about automations that misfire, bloat and price, not about missing features. Therapists already pay separately for AI notes (about $15 to $40/mo), so that problem is now a commodity.

**Meeting notes (consultants, coaches)**
- Granola: $125M Series C, Mar 2026, at a $1.5B valuation (about $192M total). Revenue is undisclosed ("2.5x since January" per the CEO). It shipped an **MCP server in Feb 2026** so agents can query notes, and is pushing into enterprise — [entrepreneurloop](https://entrepreneurloop.com/granola-ai-meeting-notes-unicorn-series-c-2026/); [okara](https://okara.ai/blog/how-granola-grew); [LeadWave](https://getleadwave.io/dossier/granola)

### Inferences
- Willingness to pay is real but anchored low: about $20 to $60/mo for "business OS" tools, and about $15 to $40/mo for single-job AI (notes). Users rebel when prices rise without new value. A new product must either be priced around **$29 to $79/mo** or tie price directly to money recovered or won.
- Review complaints ("automations that don't work", wrong PDFs attached to proposals, "AI advances that aren't very good") point to a **trust/reliability gap**. That fits an engine whose differentiator is human approval before outside effects plus an audit log.
- Meeting capture is solved and well funded (Granola, the SimplePractice Note Taker). The opening is *downstream* of notes: turning call content into a scope, proposal, change order or claim action. Granola's MCP server makes it a connector, not a competitor.

### Gaps
- Dubsado, Bonsai, Moxie and Plutio reviews and prices were not directly verified this session.
- No churn data for HoneyBook or SimplePractice after the price changes. The "exodus" is anecdotal.
- Prices and reviews for Otter and Fathom were not gathered.

## Q3. What do big AI companies and incumbents now cover well, what remains unsolved, and why?

### Takeaway
Covered: drafting text (proposals, emails) through general chat assistants, meeting transcription and summaries, therapy progress notes, and basic CRM, contracts and e-sign in all-in-one suites. Unsolved: **continuous, cross-tool monitoring that acts on the business's own commitments**, such as scope against the signed SOW and claims against payer rules. These jobs need persistent access to several private systems, the user's own history, and trustworthy approval before anything leaves the building.

### Cited Findings
- Therapy notes are commoditised. At least six vendors sell AI notes at $0.49/session to $40/mo, and the largest EHR bundles them under its BAA — [Supanote](https://www.supanote.ai/blog/best-ai-for-therapy-notes); [fast.io](https://fast.io/resources/best-ai-for-therapists-2026/)
- Meeting notes are a funded category ($1.5B Granola), now pushing into enterprise — [doolpa](https://doolpa.com/news/granola-125m-series-c-1-5b-valuation-enterprise-march-2026)
- Incumbent all-in-ones (HoneyBook) are adding AI features that users call weak ("trying too hard to roll out all AI advances that aren't very good") and automations that misfire — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/honeybook.com)
- Therapist insurance work is still manual and error-prone. Vendors describe repeated resubmissions without diagnosing enrollment root causes, and 90 to 180-day credentialing cycles — [Your Health Magazine](https://yourhealthmagazine.net/article/healthcare-administration/how-credentialing-gaps-turn-into-unpaid-claims-in-mental-health-practices/); [Clarity Health RCM](https://www.clarityhealthrcm.com/insights/insurance-credentialing-for-therapists)
- EU e-invoicing is a new compliance load on sole traders, mostly on the *receiving* side first:
  - Germany: all businesses, Freiberufler included, must be able to receive structured e-invoices since 1 Jan 2025. Issuing is mandatory from 2027 above €800k turnover and from 2028 for the rest, and Kleinunternehmer are reportedly exempt from issuing — [Solopreneurship.eu](https://solopreneurship.eu/articles/germany-e-invoicing-freelancers-2025-2028/)
  - France: all businesses, micro-entrepreneurs included, must receive from 1 Sep 2026 and SMEs and sole traders must issue from 1 Sep 2027 — [Signaturit](https://www.signaturit.com/blog/mandatory-e-invoicing-in-europe/); [facturwise](https://www.facturwise.com/en/blog/e-invoicing-deadlines-eu-country-timeline)
  - Belgium: B2B e-invoicing on Peppol BIS 3.0 has been live since 1 Jan 2026 for all VAT-registered businesses — [invoice-converter](https://www.invoice-converter.com/en/blog/europe-e-invoicing-deadlines-2026)
  - (All of these are secondary vendor sources, so check against DGFiP, BMF and FPS Finance.)

### Inferences
Why things stay unsolved, by job:
- **Scope and change control:** It needs to read the signed contract or SOW and *ongoing* client email, Slack and meeting notes, and then *decide* what counts as out of scope. Chat assistants have no persistent state across the user's tools. Suites hold the contract but do not read the conversation. Liability is low, since a human approves each change order. Mainly an **integration and trust** problem, which fits the engine.
- **Therapist insurance revenue work:** HIPAA, payer-specific rules, and clearinghouse/EDI integration. Big AI labs avoid it because of liability and regulation, while EHRs bundle basic billing. Platforms (Headway, Alma, Rula) "solve" it by taking margin. This is a **regulation plus integration** barrier, defensible but heavier.
- **EU e-invoicing:** Covered by EU accounting incumbents (sevDesk, lexoffice, Pennylane and others; unverified this session). In France, transmission must go through state-approved platforms (PA/PDP). This is a **regulation/certification** barrier, and the work sits close to accounting, which the founder rejected. Probably not a fit.
- **US quarterly taxes:** Crowded (Keeper, FlyFin, QuickBooks Solopreneur), liability-bearing, and anchored at $199 to $399/yr. Not a fit.

### Gaps
- I did not verify which EU accounting tools already handle Peppol and XRechnung receiving for freelancers, or their prices.
- No evidence on whether ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot "projects", memory or connectors now monitor email against contracts. As of the sources found, nothing indicates they do this proactively.

## Q4. Which AI-native startups are attacking these jobs, at what price, with what traction?

### Takeaway
Funding is concentrated in horizontal meeting notes (Granola) and in larger-firm consulting and proposal tools (Verra, Intellectible). Therapist AI is crowded, but almost entirely at the *notes* layer. I found **no funded AI-native player focused on scope or change-order control for solo service providers**, and none focused on solo therapists' denials and credentialing (weak search coverage, so not proof of absence).

### Cited Findings
- **Granola** (meeting notes): $125M Series C at $1.5B (Mar 2026), about $192M raised, MCP server Feb 2026. Users were about 5,000 weekly in Oct 2024 (old), with no newer disclosure — [entrepreneurloop](https://entrepreneurloop.com/granola-ai-meeting-notes-unicorn-series-c-2026/); [okara](https://okara.ai/blog/how-granola-grew)
- **Verra**: $5M seed for an "institutional memory" product aimed at consulting *firms*, claiming >80% shorter deliverable draft times (vendor claim) — [FF News](https://ffnews.com/news/verra-gets-5m-for-creating-the-institutional-memory-for-consulting-0118c0f9)
- **Intellectible** (Austin): $3M seed, Jun 2026, for AI proposal writing (target segment unclear from the snippet) — [Fundraise Insider](https://fundraiseinsider.com/blog/intellectible-raises-3m-seed-for-ai-proposal-writing/)
- **Marloo**: $10M seed for a vertical workflow product for (financial) advisers, cited as evidence that "vertical workflow products for advisers still attract money when customer proof exists" — [mean.ceo May 2026](https://blog.mean.ceo/ai-startup-funding-news-may-2026/)
- **Therapist AI notes** (prices in Q2): Upheal, Mentalyc, Blueprint, Supanote, DeepCura and others — [Supanote](https://www.supanote.ai/blog/best-ai-for-therapy-notes); [DeepCura](https://www.deepcura.com/resources/best-ai-for-therapy-notes)
- **Typical AI seed in 2026**: $3M to $4M at $16M to $18M pre-money, with EU rounds 10 to 25% lower (fundraising guide; indicative only) — [waveup](https://waveup.com/blog/how-to-raise-money-for-ai-startup/)

### Inferences
- The investor story "vertical workflow plus proof" (Marloo) is the pattern to copy: a narrow job, a measurable money outcome, a specific profession.
- Verra and Intellectible validate demand for proposals and deliverables, but upmarket. Solo and 1-to-5-person agencies are underserved by them and overserved by generic suites.

### Gaps
- I did not find traction data for Bonsai, Moxie, Plutio or Dubsado, or for any scope-creep-specific tools (e.g., stopscopecreep.com appears to be a content site, product unclear).
- No funding or traction data on AI startups for therapist insurance billing and credentialing.
- No IndieHackers revenue posts were retrieved for freelancer-tool micro-SaaS.

## Q5. Candidate opportunities: who pays, price evidence, recurrence, 3-month MVP

### Takeaway
Four viable narrow opportunities and three ruled out. The strongest fit for the existing engine (approval before outside effects, audit log, local data, MCP connectors) is **#1, Scope Guard / change-order capture for project-based creatives, developers and small agencies**, followed by **#2, discovery-call-to-SOW for solo consultants and agencies**. **#3, therapist insurance revenue rescue** has the clearest money-at-stake but a heavier regulatory build.

### Cited Findings (evidence summarised per opportunity; all cited above)

**#1 Scope Guard: detect out-of-scope requests and draft change orders**
- *Who pays:* project-based designers, developers and small agencies (1 to 5) on fixed-fee work. Weaker fit for hourly consultants.
- *Pain/money:* about 12 unpaid hrs/month and $2k to $6.5k/yr per freelancer **[vendor/unverified]** — [Damongo](https://damongo.com/how-to-stop-scope-creep-as-a-freelancer-in-2026-complete-guide-protecting-time-rates/); [stopscopecreep.com](https://stopscopecreep.com/blog/scope-creep-statistics)
- *WTP anchors:* the segment pays $36 to $129/mo for HoneyBook and balks at hikes without value — [AgencyHandy](https://www.agencyhandy.com/informative/honeybook-pricing/)
- *Competition:* HoneyBook, Dubsado and Bonsai store contracts and proposals but do not monitor conversations. Their automations draw complaints — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/honeybook.com). Granola can be an input via MCP — [okara](https://okara.ai/blog/how-granola-grew)
- *Recurrence:* weekly, on every active project.

**#2 Discovery-call to scoped proposal/SOW (using the user's own past projects and rates)**
- *Who pays:* solo consultants, fractional executives, small dev/design agencies.
- *Evidence:* funded upmarket analogues (Verra $5M, Intellectible $3M) — [FF News](https://ffnews.com/news/verra-gets-5m-for-creating-the-institutional-memory-for-consulting-0118c0f9); [Fundraise Insider](https://fundraiseinsider.com/blog/intellectible-raises-3m-seed-for-ai-proposal-writing/). Incumbent proposal flows are failing ("clients unable to sign their proposals", a wrong business's PDF attached) — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/honeybook.com)
- *Recurrence:* per lead, about weekly to monthly.

**#3 Solo/small therapy practice insurance revenue rescue (denial root-cause, resubmission prep, credentialing and re-credentialing tracker)**
- *Who pays:* in-network solo therapists and 2 to 5 clinician group practices that stay off Headway and Alma to keep rates.
- *Evidence:* 90 to 180-day credentialing, enrollment-caused denials, hours per unpaid claim — [Clarity Health RCM](https://www.clarityhealthrcm.com/insights/insurance-credentialing-for-therapists); [Blueprint](https://www.blueprint.ai/blog/insurance-claim-denials). Practices already pay $77 to $179/mo for EHRs plus $15 to $40/mo for AI notes — [TherapyDesk](https://www.therapydesk.io/blog/simplepractice-price-increase); [Supanote](https://www.supanote.ai/blog/best-ai-for-therapy-notes). Platform alternative means giving up rate (e.g., Rula about $70 to $90/hr) — [ChoosingTherapy](https://www.choosingtherapy.com/rula-insurance/)
- *Recurrence:* weekly claim batches. Credentialing renewals every few years per payer.

**#4 EU solo e-invoice receiving/compliance inbox (DE/FR/BE)**: conditional, likely rule out
- *Evidence:* the mandates are real and new for sole traders (DE receiving since Jan 2025; FR receiving Sep 2026, issuing Sep 2027; BE Jan 2026) — [Solopreneurship.eu](https://solopreneurship.eu/articles/germany-e-invoicing-freelancers-2025-2028/); [Signaturit](https://www.signaturit.com/blog/mandatory-e-invoicing-in-europe/)
- *Against:* sits next to accounting and invoicing (founder-rejected areas). French transmission requires approved platforms. EU accounting incumbents likely cover it (unverified).

**Ruled out**
- US quarterly-tax and deduction apps: crowded, liability-bearing, priced at $199 to $399/yr — [Keeper](https://www.keepertax.com/compare/keeper-vs-quickbooks)
- AI therapy progress notes / meeting notes: commoditised — [Supanote](https://www.supanote.ai/blog/best-ai-for-therapy-notes); [Granola coverage](https://entrepreneurloop.com/granola-ai-meeting-notes-unicorn-series-c-2026/)
- Upwork proposal automation: per-proposal Connects cost and platform-controlled fees make the economics platform-dependent, and automation risks breaching platform terms (ToS not verified this session) — [Monitask](https://www.monitask.com/blog/upwork-fees/)
- Invoice/receivables chasing, accounting month-end, tenders, supplier questionnaires, generic SMB agent: excluded by the founder.

### Inferences
**Feasibility for a 2 to 5 engineer team with the existing engine**

| # | Opportunity | Price point (inferred) | 3-month MVP needs | Regulation | Engine fit | Judgement |

### Gaps
- Price points in the table are **inferred** from competitor anchors, not tested.
- There is no primary evidence that freelancers will *send* change orders when prompted. The behavioural barrier (fear of upsetting clients) may dominate the tooling problem. This needs interviews.
- For #3, I did not verify clearinghouse API options (e.g., Availity, Office Ally, Stedi), CAQH API access, or competing credentialing services and their prices.
- Coaches, tutors, translators and creators were not researched in depth this session. No specific evidence was gathered on their distinct pains (e.g., tutor no-shows, translator CAT-tool or agency rate pressure, creator brand-deal contracts). Treat these sub-segments as unexplored.
